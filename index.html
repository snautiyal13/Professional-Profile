Story: From Technology Enthusiast to Global Automotive Leader

I am a hands-on, matrix-driven executive with a lifelong passion for technology and how it transforms the automotive value chain. My career spans leading IT and software portfolios, selling enterprise-wide licenses, and delivering engineering services for test, measurement, validation, and compliance. I built my foundation at National Instruments, expanding into enterprise licensing with automotive and industrial stakeholders, and I continued this trajectory at Panasonic, where I grew into the leadership role that now steers the Automotive & Mobility division.

At Panasonic, I led multi‑disciplinary teams across the Americas, Europe, and Asia, aligning product roadmap with vehicle architectures—ADAS, infotainment, connectivity, and software-defined vehicle strategies. I champion long-cycle, platform-level design-wins, executive governance, and disciplined pipeline management to convert complex opportunities into sustainable revenue. My journey demonstrates that technology leadership, combined with a relentless focus on customer value, can redefine enterprise risk, supply resilience, and go-to-market velocity in a rapidly evolving automotive economy.
